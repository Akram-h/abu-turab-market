**Full description:**
Al-Hamam Educational Platform helps schools follow each student and keeps parents, teachers and administration connected.

For parents: student profile and monthly fee installments (private schools), evaluations, grades, attendance, contact teachers by call or WhatsApp, academic and administrative notices, e-lessons, online quizzes, homework, weekly timetable, exam schedule, library, videos, events and year archive.

For schools: classes and sections with separate boys and girls shifts, separate accounts for principal, teachers and parents, Excel upload of students and teachers with printable login lists, and full separation between schools.

Note: accounts are created by your school. Contact your school to receive your username and password.
